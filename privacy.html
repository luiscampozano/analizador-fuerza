Privacy policy

FuerzaPro · Last updated: November 17, 2026 · Español
In short: FuerzaPro processes your videos on your phone, does not sell your data, shows no ads and only stores information in the cloud if you sign in to sync.

This is an English translation. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Who is responsible

FuerzaPro is an app developed by Ing. Luis Campozano, MBA (Ecuador). For any privacy question you can write to fuerzapro2026@gmail.com.

2. What data is used and why

  • Videos of your sets: they are analyzed with artificial intelligence on your phone. Videos are not uploaded to any server, unless you choose to post one in the community or ask for the AI explanation (see below).
  • Workouts, routines, records, analyses and meals: stored on your phone. If you sign in, they are copied to the cloud to sync them between your devices and keep a backup.
  • Body and profile data (weight, height, age, sex, photo and the name you enter): used to calculate your metrics (for example, DOTS points and nutrition goals) and treated like the rest of your data.
  • Community (optional): if you create a public profile, your username, display name, photo, gym, category and description are public to FuerzaPro users, along with the workouts, analyses, photos and videos you choose to share (with their likes and comments). Community photos and videos are stored on Cloudflare R2; videos are deleted automatically after 1 year. We also store who you follow, who you block and the reports you send (reports are anonymous to the reported person).
  • Email account: if you create your account with email and password, we store your name and email. The password is never stored: only an encrypted fingerprint (PBKDF2) that does not allow recovering it.
  • Google account: if you choose «Continue with Google», we receive your name, email and profile photo to identify your account. We do not access your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • Apple account (iPhone): if you choose «Continue with Apple», we receive from Apple an identifier for your account and, the first time, your name and email (which may be a private Apple relay email if you choose to hide it).
  • Food search: when you search for a product or scan a barcode, the text or code is sent to Open Food Facts (an open database) to get its nutrition information.
  • AI nutrition and coach features (optional, with your permission): the first time you use an AI feature we explain what is sent and ask for your permission; you can withdraw it in Profile. If you use «meal photo», «write what I ate», «read the label with AI», «Which machine is it?», the AI nutritionist menu or the AI coach, the photo or text (and, for the coach and the menu, a summary of your workouts, meals, goals and profile data —age, sex, weight, height and goal—, and for the menu also your tastes, what you avoid and your workout of the day) is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to get the estimate or the advice. FuerzaPro does not store those photos; it only counts how many times you use each feature to apply the limits. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Plan and usage: we store your plan and how many analyses and AI credits you use each month, to apply each plan's limits.
  • Subscriptions: paid plans are charged through Google Play on Android and through Apple (App Store) on iPhone; they process the payment and we never see your card. We only store the purchase identifier the store gives us, the plan, its expiration and whether it renews, linked to your account. On iPhone, the purchase carries an anonymous code of your FuerzaPro account (not your email) so the plan is assigned to your account.
  • Anonymous usage count: once a day the app sends a random identifier created on your phone (not your email or account), whether you use it from the Google Play app, the web or iPhone, and whether you are signed in. It is only used to know how many people use FuerzaPro.
  • Apple Health (optional, iPhone app only): if you connect it in Profile → ⚙️ Settings, FuerzaPro saves your workouts to Apple Health (start and end time, and estimated calories) and reads your latest weight and height. What we read from Apple Health stays on your phone: it only goes to your FuerzaPro profile (and to your cloud copy, if you have an account) when you tap «Use Apple Health's». We never use Apple Health data for advertising or share it with third parties. You can disconnect it in the app and remove the permission in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access → FuerzaPro.
  • Rest reminders (optional, iPhone app): if you turn them on, the iPhone shows a local notification when your rest ends; it does not go through the internet.
  • Where you use your account from: if you are signed in, once a day we store with your account whether you use it from the Android app, the iPhone app or the web (only that: not the phone model or your location), to provide better support and know which platforms our users are on. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • AI explanation of a set (optional): if you tap «Explain with AI» on an analysis, the measured numbers of that set and up to 4 frames taken from the telemetry video are sent to Anthropic (Claude), only to write the explanation. We ask you to confirm before sending them. FuerzaPro does not store those frames; the explanation is saved with the analysis (on your phone and, if you are signed in, in your cloud copy).
  • Error reports (optional): if you correct the rep count of a set and agree to send the case, we receive the exercise, your correction, your comment and the body points the AI detected frame by frame (coordinates, no image or video). We use them only to improve detection and they are deleted if you delete your account.
  • Feedback and suggestions (optional): if you use «Send feedback or suggestion» in Profile, we receive your message and, if you attach it, a screenshot, along with your account so we can reply.
  • Camera: used to record your sets, live mode and read barcodes; those images are processed on the phone. If you take a photo for an AI feature (meal, label or machine), that photo is sent to Anthropic as explained above.

Why we can use your data (legal basis). Under Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP):

  • To provide the service you ask for (performance of the contract you accept by using the app): your account, the cloud copy of what you log (health data, in addition, only with the permission below), your plan and usage limits, and subscriptions.
  • With your explicit consent for health data (sensitive data under the LOPDP): your weight, height, age, sex and body measurements, your workouts and video analyses, and your meals, goals and what you don't eat. Before using them for the first time we ask for this permission separately. You can withdraw it in Profile («Withdraw health data permission»): your data stays saved, but the app stops using it for new analyses or logs until you accept again; the cloud copy stays in sync while you are signed in. To delete it, «Delete account and cloud data» deletes the cloud copy, and on the phone you can uninstall the app or clear the site data.
  • With your consent, which you can withdraw at any time: AI features, the community (public profile and posts), sending error reports, technical diagnostics and the feedback you send us from the app.
  • Legitimate interest, minimally: the anonymous usage count, where you use your account from (Android, iPhone or web) and the security of the service (preventing abuse).

3. Where it is stored

Synced data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (D1 database and, for community photos and videos, R2), over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Data that is only on your phone stays in the browser's or app's storage.

International transfer. The providers we use (Cloudflare, Anthropic, Google, Apple, Open Food Facts and jsDelivr, which delivers the AI models that run on your phone) have servers in several countries, mainly in the United States and Europe. That is why, when you use the cloud, AI features, sign-in or food search, your data may be transferred outside Ecuador. We only send what each feature needs, always over an encrypted connection. Cloudflare, Anthropic, Google and Apple are contractually committed to protect the data and not use it for other purposes (for example, under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models); Open Food Facts is a public database and only receives the text or code you search for.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and your cloud data (workouts, meals, analyses, profile, plan, usage, purchases, feedback sent and posts): as long as you have the account. When you delete it, they are deleted immediately; the provider's database recovery history (Cloudflare D1) expires on its own within 30 days at most. Foods you added to the shared database remain available to everyone, without your name.
  • Photos and text you send to the AI: FuerzaPro does not store them. The result (the logged meal or the set explanation) is saved with your data and synced if you are signed in. Anthropic deletes them from its systems within 30 days at most, except for the security exceptions set out in its terms.
  • Error reports and diagnostics: as long as you have the account; deleted when you delete it or if you ask us.
  • Purchase data (purchase identifier, plan and dates): as long as you have the account; deleted when you delete it. Google Play and Apple, which process the payment, keep their own records under their policies.
  • Usage count: for each phone and day we store the random identifier created on your phone, the platform and whether you were signed in, without your account, email or IP. Since it cannot be linked to your account, it is not deleted when you delete it; to stop sending it, clear the app's data.
  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

4. Who it is shared with

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers. Only the providers needed for the app to work are involved: Google and Apple (sign-in and subscription billing), Cloudflare (hosting, database and files), Anthropic (AI nutrition, coach and set explanation features, only when you use them) and Open Food Facts (food searches). In the community, only what you choose to post is public, and only people signed in to FuerzaPro can see it. See the community guidelines.

5. Your rights and how to delete your data

  • You can export all your data at any time from Profile → Save backup.
  • You can delete your account and all data stored in the cloud (including your community activity) from Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you no longer have the app, follow the steps in Delete your account.
  • You can leave only the community (your public profile, posts, comments and likes are deleted) from your public profile → ⋯ → Leave the community.
  • To delete the data on your phone, uninstall the app or clear the site data in your browser.
  • You can also request access, correction or deletion by writing to the email above, under Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection.

Under the LOPDP you have the right to:

  • Information about how and why we use your data (this policy).
  • Free access to your data, without having to justify the request.
  • Rectification and update of incorrect or incomplete data (you can edit most of it yourself in Profile).
  • Deletion of your data (Profile → Delete account, or by email).
  • Objection and suspension of processing: for example, withdrawing the AI or health data permission in Profile, or leaving the community.
  • Portability: receiving your data in a structured, machine-readable format (Profile → Save full backup, in JSON, with what is stored on your phone; workouts can also be exported to Excel if your plan includes it; for the rest of your cloud data, write to us and we will send it free of charge).
  • Not to be subject to decisions based solely or partly on automated assessments, including profiling, that affect you: the app's estimates are for guidance only and are not used to make decisions about you.
  • Free consultation with Ecuador's National Registry of Personal Data Protection.
  • File a complaint with Ecuador's Superintendency of Personal Data Protection if you believe we did not handle your request properly.

We answer requests sent to fuerzapro2026@gmail.com within 15 days at most, as required by law.

6. Minors

FuerzaPro is intended for people over 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Health

The app's estimates (velocity, RIR, 1RM, calories) are for guidance only and do not replace the advice of a doctor, nutritionist or coach.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and let you know in the app.
