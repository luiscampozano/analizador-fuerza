Community guidelines

FuerzaPro · Last updated: October 26, 2026 · Español
The FuerzaPro community is for sharing workouts, learning and motivating each other. By creating your public profile you accept these guidelines. If you don't follow them, we may remove your content or remove you from the community.

1. Respect above all

  • No insults or mocking of other people's bodies, strength or level.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, threats and hate speech (based on origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other condition).
  • Do not post other people's personal data (phone numbers, addresses, photos without permission).

2. Allowed content

  • Your workouts, records, video analyses and comments about training, technique and nutrition.
  • Sexual or violent content, spam, unsolicited advertising, scams and misleading links are not allowed.
  • Do not promote banned substances, doping, dangerous practices or extreme diets.
  • Do not post false data on purpose or impersonate another person.

3. You control what you share

  • Nothing is posted on its own: only what you share with the «📣 Share» button.
  • You can edit or delete your posts and comments whenever you want, and delete comments on your posts.
  • You can leave the community from your public profile (your profile, posts, comments and likes are deleted) or delete your whole account from Profile.

4. Report and block

  • On every post, comment or profile you have the ⋯ menu to report or block.
  • When you block someone, you won't see their content, they won't be able to see yours or comment, and you stop following each other.
  • Reports are anonymous. Content with several reports is hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it. We review reports and act within 24 hours at most.

5. Safety

FuerzaPro's data (velocity, RPE, estimated 1RM) is for guidance only. Do not follow advice from other people that puts your health at risk; consult a professional. You must be at least 16 to use the community.

6. Contact

To report something urgent or appeal a decision, write to fuerzapro2026@gmail.com.
