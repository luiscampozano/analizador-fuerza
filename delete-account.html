Delete your FuerzaPro account

FuerzaPro · Ing. Luis Campozano, MBA · Last updated: November 3, 2026 · Español
You can delete your FuerzaPro account and all your data at any time, whether or not you have the app installed. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Option 1 · From the app or the web (immediate)

If you no longer have the app installed, open FuerzaPro from your phone's or computer's browser: it works the same.

Open FuerzaPro
  1. Sign in with the same account (Google, Apple or email and password).
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap 🗑️ Delete account and cloud data.
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE.

Option 2 · By email

If you can't sign in, write to fuerzapro2026@gmail.com with the subject «Delete my account», from the same email as your account. We will delete it within 30 days at most and confirm by email.

Delete only some data (without deleting the account)

You can delete individual data from the app whenever you want. When you delete it, it is also removed from the cloud:

  • A workout: Train tab → open the workout → Delete.
  • An analyzed set (with its report and video): open it from your history → Delete.
  • A cardio or sports analysis: open it → Delete.
  • A meal: Nutrition tab → tap the meal → Delete.
  • A community post (with its photos, video, likes and comments): on the post → ⋯ → Delete.
  • All your community activity: your public profile → ⋯ → Leave the community (your profile, posts, comments and likes are deleted; your workouts are not touched).

You can also ask us to delete specific data by writing to fuerzapro2026@gmail.com from your account's email, saying which data you want deleted. We do it within 30 days at most.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email, photo and the encrypted fingerprint of your password, and all your open sessions.
  • Everything synced to the cloud: workouts, routines, records, video analyses, meals, body and profile data.
  • Your community activity: public profile, posts (with their photos and videos), comments, likes, followers and reports.
  • The rep count error reports you sent, your plan, your purchase records and your usage counters.

What is kept

  • Foods you added to the shared database stay for everyone, without your name.
  • The database's technical backups (Cloudflare) renew on their own and disappear within 30 days at most.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → FuerzaPro. Google keeps its own record of your payments.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription either. On your iPhone, cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FuerzaPro. Apple keeps its own record of your payments.
  • What is stored on your own phone is not deleted from the cloud: to remove it, uninstall the app or clear the site data in your browser.
